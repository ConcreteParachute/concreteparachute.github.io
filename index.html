Debut Single Jan 08

Torrent

A hard-hitting anthem exploring control, subtle manipulation, and the turning point of reclaiming agency. Whether breaking free from abusive dynamics, overbearing authority, or ideological indoctrination, "Torrent" captures the moment doubt transforms into defiance.

Won’t let your torrent swallow me.

Drifting Awake

An atmospheric post-hardcore track tackling dissociation and the weight of living on autopilot. "Drifting Awake" depicts the constant tug-of-war between being trapped in memory or future anxiety, and those brief, sudden moments of lucid clarity in the present.

The Band

Atlanta, GA • Heavy Melodic Rock

Emerging from Griffin, Georgia, Concrete Parachute delivers an impact-driven blend of heavy melodic rock and post-hardcore. Fusing fast guitar riffage with infectious clean dual-lead vocal hooks and aggressive screaming accents, the quartet captures heavy rock intensity while remaining catchy and radio-accessible.

Lineup

  • Chad Askew – Lead Guitar / Co-Lead Vocals
  • Brandon Halvorsen – Rhythm Guitar / Co-Lead Vocals
  • Josh Hollums – Bass
  • Michael Hayes – Drums

Influences

Drawing heavily from the 2000s post-hardcore and active rock movements, including early A Day To Remember, Story of the Year, Chevelle, and Breaking Benjamin.
