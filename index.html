About Concrete Parachute: Atlanta Heavy Rock Band

Atlanta, GA • Heavy Melodic Rock & Post-Hardcore

Emerging from Griffin, Georgia and rooted in the vibrant Atlanta music scene, Concrete Parachute delivers an impact-driven blend of heavy melodic rock and post-hardcore. Fusing fast guitar riffage with infectious clean dual-lead vocal hooks and aggressive screaming accents, the quartet captures heavy rock intensity while remaining catchy and radio-accessible.

Based in Atlanta, Georgia and available to book across the Southeast, the band's debut singles "Torrent" (January 8, 2027) and "Drifting Awake" (February 19, 2027) are the first chapter of the Concrete Parachute story. Fans of A Day To Remember, Story of the Year, Chevelle and Breaking Benjamin will find familiar ground: big hooks, heavy riffs and honest lyrics. See tour dates, grab merch or book the band.

Band Members

  • Chad Askew – Lead Guitar / Co-Lead Vocals
  • Brandon Halvorsen – Rhythm Guitar / Co-Lead Vocals
  • Josh Hollums – Bass
  • Michael Hayes – Drums

Musical Influences

Drawing heavily from the 2000s post-hardcore and active rock movements, including early A Day To Remember, Story of the Year, Chevelle, and Breaking Benjamin.

Concrete Parachute FAQ

Releases, Booking, Tour & Merch

Who is Concrete Parachute?

Concrete Parachute is a four-piece heavy melodic rock and post-hardcore band from Atlanta, Georgia, formed in Griffin, Georgia. The lineup is Chad Askew (lead guitar, co-lead vocals), Brandon Halvorsen (rhythm guitar, co-lead vocals), Josh Hollums (bass) and Michael Hayes (drums).

When does Concrete Parachute's debut single "Torrent" come out?

"Torrent" is released January 8, 2027, with a music video premiering the same day. Follow the band on Spotify, subscribe on YouTube, or pre-order the digital bundle on Bandcamp.

When is the second single "Drifting Awake" released?

"Drifting Awake", an atmospheric post-hardcore follow-up single, is scheduled for February 19, 2027.

What kind of music does Concrete Parachute play?

Heavy melodic rock and post-hardcore: fast guitar riffs, clean dual-lead vocal hooks and aggressive screamed accents. Influences include early A Day To Remember, Story of the Year, Chevelle and Breaking Benjamin.

Where can I find Concrete Parachute tour dates and tickets?

Live dates and ticket links appear in the Tour section of this page and are synced with the band's Bandsintown profile. Follow the band on Bandsintown to be notified when new shows are announced.

Email booking@concreteparachute.com, call 404-855-0849, or use the booking form on this page. The band is booking opening support slots, festival showcases and headlining local bills in Atlanta, Georgia and the Southeast US.
