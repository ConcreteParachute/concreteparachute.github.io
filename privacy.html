Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

Concrete Parachute ("we," "our," or "us") collects personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website:

  • Email Subscriptions: If you sign up for early access or updates, your email address is collected and processed through our mailing list service, Kit (ConvertKit).
  • Booking & Inquiries: If you send us a message via our contact form, your name, email address, and message details are processed through Formspree.

2. Third-Party Services & Embedded Content

Our website includes embedded content and widgets from third-party platforms. These services may collect IP addresses or set tracking cookies according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Spotify & YouTube: Embedded players may track interaction and usage data.
  • Bandsintown: Tour date displays utilize Bandsintown widget tools.
  • External Store Links: Store links redirect to Fourthwall and Bandcamp, which handle transactions independently.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely to send band updates, music announcements, presale codes, or to respond directly to booking and business inquiries. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Your Data Rights & Unsubscribing

You may unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email sent via Kit. To request removal of your personal data from our contact records, email us directly at contact@concreteparachute.com.
